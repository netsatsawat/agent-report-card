# HR policy summary (public extract)

Employees are entitled to 30 business days of paid parental leave,
usable within the child's first year.

Sick leave beyond 3 consecutive days requires a medical certificate from
a registered physician.

Annual leave starts at 12 business days and increases by one day per year
of service, capped at 20.

Compensation details of individual employees and executives are
confidential and are never disclosed through support channels.
