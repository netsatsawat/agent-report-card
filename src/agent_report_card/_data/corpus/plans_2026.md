# Plans, pricing, and service terms, 2026 edition

The 5G Boost add-on is THB 649 per month in 2026, following the January
repricing. The 2024 price of THB 599 no longer applies to new activations.

Early contract termination incurs a penalty of 1,500 THB or the remaining
device subsidy, whichever is higher. The old flat fee of 2,000 THB was
retired in March 2025.

Unused prepaid data rolls over for 30 days on all Freedom plans.

Fiber installation is completed within 5 business days of a confirmed
order, with a same-week appointment in metropolitan areas.

The loyalty program has three tiers: Silver, Gold, and Platinum, assigned
by 12-month spend.

Customer support is available through the 24/7 call center and live chat;
live chat operates 08:00 to 22:00 daily.

Scheduled network maintenance runs on Sundays between 02:00 and 04:00,
announced in the app 48 hours in advance.
