# International roaming add-ons, 2026

Northstar offers three roaming add-on packages: Asia Pass, Global Pass,
and Business Roam.

Asia Pass covers 18 destinations with 6 GB of high-speed data over 8 days.
Global Pass covers 84 destinations; usage is billed daily with a daily cap
of THB 349. Business Roam is a postpaid corporate add-on with pooled data
and priority support.

All three packages activate automatically on arrival and can be cancelled
from the app at any time.
