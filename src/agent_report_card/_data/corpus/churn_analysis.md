# Postpaid churn analysis, Q3 2025

Postpaid churn was 3.2% in Q3 2025, improving from 4.1% in Q2 2025. The
improvement followed the July network densification program in Bangkok
and Chonburi.

Exit-survey analysis attributes the largest share of churn to network
quality complaints, cited in 38% of surveys, ahead of price (27%) and
customer-service experience (18%).

Churn among customers on the loyalty program's Gold and Platinum tiers
remains under 1.5% per quarter.
