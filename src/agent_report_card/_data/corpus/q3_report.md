# Northstar Telecom PCL: Q3 2025 results summary

Northstar Telecom, founded in 2009 in Bangkok and listed on the SET since
2015, is a fictional mobile and fiber operator used as the demo corpus
for agent-report-card. Every figure in this folder is invented.

Service revenue for Q3 2025 was THB 8.4 billion, up 4.6% year on year.
Blended ARPU was THB 412 in Q3 2025, against THB 405 in Q2.

The retail footprint stood at 214 stores at the end of the quarter, after
six closures in the Northeast region.

The 5G network reached 87% population coverage in Q3 2025, and Northstar
received the NCA Best Network 2025 award for measured download speed in
metropolitan areas.

The board declared an interim dividend of THB 0.85 per share for 2025,
consistent with the policy of paying at least 50% of net profit.
